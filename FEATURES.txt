# Pokemon FireRed Evolved

## Pokemon and Pokedex
- All 251 Gen1 and Gen2 Pokemon are obtainable.
- FireRed and LeafGreen wild rosters are combined.
- Gen1 starters have wild encounters after Normal Champion.
- Rare trade-only Kanto species have wild habitats.
- Gen2 wild Pokemon are available before Champion.
- G/S/C Kanto species appear in equivalent habitats.
- Gen2 placement respects original encounter levels; Skarmory appears at Lv27.
- Gen2 Safari imports use Emerald levels.
- Altering Cave supports Nintendo's eight Gen2 species.
- Original FireRed Sevii Gen2 encounters are restored.
- Mixed wild pools preserve a 50/50 generation split.
- Generation pools with 5+ species halve rates of 24% or higher.
- Rare rates have a 5% floor within each generation.
- Pokedex entries, sorting, searches and counts stop at #251.
- Gen2 entries, capture pages and Summary/Hall of Fame numbers appear early.
- Pokedex completion checks count only caught #1-#251.
- Oak's completion ratings, diploma and national card checks require all 251.
- Bookcases reveal up to three new Gen2 seen entries once each.
- Bookcase hints exclude Mew and Celebi.
- Gen2 habitat maps work before National Dex.
- Separate encounter and acquisition guides cover all 251 Pokemon.

## Walking Pokemon
- All 251 Pokemon have walking followers and shiny palettes.
- Followers match the nighttime color rules for other characters.
- Followers face their travel direction and keep up while running and turning.
- Your first healthy, non-Egg party member follows on foot.
- Talk to your partner for cries, emotions and contextual reactions.
- Friendship, health, weather, location and day/night affect reactions.
- Viridian Pokemon Center offers a saved follower toggle.

## Evolution and training
- All Gen2 evolutions work before National Dex.
- Bill offers animated trade-back evolutions at his Cerulean-area house.
- Level37 trade-evolution alternatives remain available.
- Held-item trade evolutions consume their required item.
- Sun Stone evolves Eevee into Espeon; Moon Stone produces Umbreon.
- Friendship Espeon uses 4:00 AM-5:59 PM; Umbreon uses 6:00 PM-3:59 AM.
- Stone and Gen2 evolutions offer missed level-up moves.
- SELECT on Summary Skills cycles Stats, IVs and EVs.
- Repels last longer and offer automatic reuse prompts.
- Magma Armor and Flame Body speed up egg hatching.
- Move tutors can teach repeatedly; normal eligibility and costs remain.
- Celadon sells 32 TMs, including the 26 additions at custom prices.
- Celadon also sells Sun Stones and Moon Stones.
- Mt. Moon and Pokemon Mansion have visible Sun Stone pickups.
- Incoming trades reject Gen3 species and introduced items.

- Gift nickname prompts identify the correct Pokemon.

## World and exploration
- Real-time day/night affects lighting, encounters, weather and daily events.
- Saved DAY/NIGHT options override effective daylight.
- Selected NPC dialogue changes with the time of day.
- Outdoor weather includes restored effects and Ember Spa fog.
- Kanto and Frontier windows light correctly at night.
- Pyramid lights are limited to its doorways.
- Menus, interiors and battles retain their normal colors.
- Dialogue text runs at FAST speed.
- Initial RNG seeding follows Ruby/Sapphire while RTC features remain.
- TREE SHAKER brings G/S/C large-tree encounters to Kanto.
- Tree encounters mix generations without a Champion switch.
- Night trees give Ghost types a combined 25% share.
- Tree sleepers follow Gen2 species/time rules with seven initial sleep turns.
- Gastly has a 5% nighttime share in every grassy wild-encounter area.
- Misdreavus uses Lv45 in suitable late-game trees.
- Tree interactions work on every side, including Viridian Forest.
- Strength retains its original boulder behavior.
- Acro Bike supports wheelies, bunnyhops and jumping ledges both ways.
- Red and Green have matching Acro Bike sprites.
- Boats return the player clear of Vermilion's sailor.

## Legends and postgame
- Mew waits under the truck after catching #1-#150.
- The truck moves without the removed truck sound.
- Faraway Island unlocks after catching #1-#250 and Normal Champion.
- Celebi uses Emerald's Mew hide-and-seek game and Radical Red walking art.
- Faraway Island has proper tall-grass effects, cutting and music.
- Legendary encounters remain available until caught.
- One standard beast roams; the other two replace caught birds after Normal Champion.
- The roaming beast appears in grass and faces the player.
- Roamers relocate after fainting or escaping; Roar no longer retires them.
- Roaming Pokemon receive proper IVs.
- Snorlax stays until caught.
- Route Sudowoodo needs Normal Champion, both Snorlax encounters and its own Snorlax caught.
- Frontier Sudowoodo is independent and stays until caught.
- Oak's Challenge requires Hard Champion and the existing Mewtwo prerequisite.
- Oak leaves his lab until the challenge is beaten; JAIME gives a Cerulean hint.
- Beating Oak's Challenge awards MYSTIC TICKET for Ho-Oh/Lugia at Navel Rock.
- Oak's ratings, welcome, regional reminders and reward dialogue are cleaned up.
- The sailor offers one ferry menu and lets ticket holders walk past.
- Ferry destinations follow the truck requirement, passes and island tickets.

## Battle Frontier
- Normal Champion unlocks the Battle Frontier.
- Tower, Factory, Dome, Arena, Palace, Pike and Pyramid are implemented.
- Original rules, lobbies, NPCs, menus, records and facility music are restored.
- Singles, doubles, Level50 and Open Level modes use supported Gen1/2 teams.
- Factory supports rental selection, swaps, streaks and Noland.
- Tower supports NPC partners, apprentices and Anabel.
- Dome supports brackets, opponent details, championships and Tucker.
- Arena judging, Palace autonomy and Pike room events are retained.
- Pyramid keeps its floors, exploration and private challenge bag.
- Frontier Brains, BP rewards and Silver/Gold symbols are available.
- Tower link/wireless multi battles and battle recordings are supported.
- Frontier Pass shows records and supports recording playback.
- Original parties return safely after challenges and playback.
- Invalid or oversized recordings are rejected without losing the previous recording.
